Device serial and operator identity are withheld from these artifacts to protect user privacy. All evidence files were collected from a single operator-owned Amazon Fire HD 10 (2021, 11th gen, trona/KFTRWI) device during the 2026-10-12 root verification campaign. Device timestamps are recorded in UTC from the ADB host and correspond to real test runs during which successful exploitation of CVE-2024-31317, CVE-2019-2181, and the Amazon time_update service was demonstrated. The campaign captured the complete root chain including kernel leak, SELinux flip, and uid=0 debloat operations, verified by the RETRY_LOOP_WIN success marker in verification-run.log (the campaign automation's log) and zero kernel faults in the pstore audit.
